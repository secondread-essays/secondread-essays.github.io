What we work on

Every part of the application that moves the decision.

Your Early Decision pickEarly Decision admit rates at many top schools run far above Regular Decision. Choosing the right ED school is often the single biggest lever you have, and most students choose by reputation alone.

The major and college you apply toAt universities that admit by college or major, the box you tick changes your odds. We pick the one that's honest to your interests and gives you the best shot.

What a markup looks like

Comments in the margin of your draft, and the three changes that matter most.

Personal statement · Draft 1412 of 650 words

I have always been passionate about technology.1 In SS22, I was elected President of the Science Club, where I organized the school's first coding competition with over 120 participants3. I also won second place at the Lagos State Mathematics Olympiad and volunteered at a community tutoring center. These experiences taught me leadership, perseverance, and the importance of giving back.4

Top change: this paragraph is a résumé in sentences. Cut it to one line and spend the space on the night the competition almost fell apart.

George1
Readers see this opening thousands of times. Open inside the moment instead. Try starting with what was on your screen at 2 a.m. the night before.
George2
US readers won't know "SS2." Write "eleventh grade (SS2)" once.
George3
Strong number. Move it to your activities list: "Founded school's first coding contest; 120 competitors, 9 schools."
George4
Three virtues in one line reads like a list. Keep "perseverance" and prove it with the scene above.
